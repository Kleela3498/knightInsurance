🧪 Edge-Case Lab

What is this? A safety drill. It takes the sample submissions, deliberately breaks something (the AI goes down, gives nonsense, a PDF is unreadable, a broker tries to trick the AI…) and checks that the app stays safe and still gives the right answer.

How to read it: ✓ Pass means the app handled the problem correctly. Click any row to see what was broken, why it matters, what the app did, and the summary and email the underwriter would still get. No live AI model is called: a scripted stand-in plays the model, so this tests the rule logic and the fallbacks without Ollama, and nothing is saved to your history.
